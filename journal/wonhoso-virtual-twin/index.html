Wonhoso: One Identity, Human Creativity and a Virtual Twin

By Etherial Agency · 14 October 2026 · Agency editorial

A digital appearance is only one part of an artist’s story. Understanding Wonhoso begins with the person behind that appearance, then with the creative decisions that give his music its direction.

One identity across two forms

At Etherial Agency, Virtual Twin means the virtual counterpart of the same real person who operates it. Wonhoso’s physical and digital expressions share one identity. His work brings human performance together with AI-assisted production.

That distinction matters when discovering his music. “Virtual Twin” describes the relationship between an artist and his digital counterpart; “AI-assisted” describes tools used in production. These terms answer different questions. A digital image alone does not explain who made a work, how it was performed or which decisions shaped it.

Creative choices give the tools direction

In his written interview published by Jason Edwards Magazine, Wonhoso describes a process that can begin with a phrase, a feeling, an image or a melody. He explains that technology helps him explore musical textures and arrangements, while his own judgment determines which direction fits the work.

His account places emotion ahead of a fixed genre label. Experimentation offers possibilities; choosing what to retain gives those possibilities an artistic purpose. These are his descriptions of his overall approach, rather than a breakdown of individual credits for every recording.

Listening to Together

In the same interview, Wonhoso presents Together through the idea of connection: different people and forms of expression can coexist without becoming identical. The song extends his interest in what brings people closer.

For a first encounter with his work, start with the official TOGETHER music video, then read his account of the creative process. Listening and reading offer complementary ways to explore the relationship between the music, its intention and the artist’s digital expression.
