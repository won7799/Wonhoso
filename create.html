FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things companies ask us before starting a project.

Who owns the artist we create?
The client always owns the artist created for them.
Does the artist have to be Asian or Korean?
No. Any culture, ethnicity or style.
What’s the difference between Virtual Twin, Hybrid and 100% AI?
A Virtual Twin shares the identity of the real person who operates it. A Hybrid Artist is a distinct AI character brought to life by human performers. A 100% AI character is a fully generated identity, shaped by human creators. See the identity guide ↗
Is the music original and licensed for commercial use?
Original music can be part of a project. How it may be used is agreed for that project.
How do you handle AI disclosure on social platforms?
It depends on the client. Fully AI artists carry the platform’s AI-generated label. Real people with an AI virtual twin — a Virtual Twin on this site — are not labeled as AI.
How long does it take and what does it cost?
Every project is quoted to its scope after a discovery call; we don’t publish fixed prices. Typical timeline: 4–16 weeks, depending on the package. Request a quote.
Can you work with our existing talent?
A Virtual Twin is the digital counterpart of a real person. Tell us about the talent and we will confirm what that project can include.
