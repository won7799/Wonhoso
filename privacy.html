Etherial Agency / Privacy

Privacy.

Last updated 20 October 2026. Etherial Agency, Seoul.

Who we are

Etherial Agency is based in Seoul, South Korea. Questions about personal information: legal@etherialagency.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send by email to partnerships@etherialagency.com, such as your name, company, message and anything else you include.

Our website hosting provider may keep standard technical logs such as an IP address and browser type. We do not run our own analytics product on this site.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to inquiries and to discuss partnerships. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any legal obligation, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Email legal@etherialagency.com. We handle these requests with Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) in mind and, where it applies, the rights described in the GDPR. This page does not claim a certification or a specific legal registration.

Contact

Etherial Agency, Seoul, South Korea. legal@etherialagency.com.
