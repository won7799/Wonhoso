Wonhoso

Virtual Twin · Music · Performance · Culture

Wonhoso is a Virtual Twin: his digital counterpart and the real person behind it share one identity, expressed across physical and digital worlds.

Understanding Wonhoso’s identity

What does Virtual Twin mean?

A Virtual Twin is the digital counterpart of the same real person who operates it. For Wonhoso, the human artist and his digital expression share one identity.

Is Wonhoso a fully AI-generated character?

No. Wonhoso is a Virtual Twin of a real person. AI-assisted production is part of how his work is made; it does not mean that his identity is a wholly generated fictional character.

How are human performance and AI involved?

Wonhoso’s work combines human performance and creative direction with AI-assisted production. The artist’s identity and the tools used to produce a work are different things.

Wonhoso의 정체성

Wonhoso는 실제 인물 본인과 동일한 정체성을 지닌 버추얼 트윈입니다. 사람인 아티스트와 디지털 모습은 하나의 정체성으로 현실과 디지털 세계에서 활동합니다. 그의 작업은 사람의 퍼포먼스와 AI 보조 제작을 결합합니다. 완전히 생성된 가상의 인물이 아닙니다.
