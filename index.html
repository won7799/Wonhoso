About Etherial

Etherial is an entertainment and technology agency. In simple terms: we build artists, and we build the tech that powers them.

Virtual Twin Artist. A real person, real face, real voice, whose content we enhance and produce through AI editing. The artist is a genuine human; AI is how their content gets made, faster and at a scale a normal production schedule can't match.
Full AI creators. A character built entirely with AI. No real person behind the face. Same quality bar, same consistency, same ability to headline a brand campaign.

On the entertainment side, we manage artists, produce music, and build out full idol acts. On the technology side, we produce video ads, build AI-powered apps, and design and build websites, for brands, for creators, and for our own roster.
