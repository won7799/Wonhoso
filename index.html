Etherial is a talent and production agency built around AI. We work with two kinds of creators, and it's worth explaining the difference clearly:

Virtual twins (AI Hybrid). A real person, with a real face and a real voice, whose content is produced through AI tools. The creator is a genuine human; the AI is how their content gets made, faster and at a scale a normal production schedule can't match.
Full AI creators. A character built entirely with AI, with no real person behind the face. Same quality bar, same consistency, same ability to headline a brand campaign.

Either way, brands get consistent, camera-ready talent without the scheduling risk of a traditional influencer, and creators get professional video production without needing a camera crew. We also produce original music for the creators on our roster.

Beyond ad campaigns, we also produce mini k-dramas and mini documentaries starring our creators, plus advertising and brand campaigns for companies who want our talent fronting their marketing. And yes, we also build custom websites, like this one.
