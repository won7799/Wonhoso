Understanding artist identitiesA guide to our creative identities+
01

Virtual Twin

A virtual counterpart of the same real person who operates it. The virtual model and the person share one identity, expressed across real and digital worlds.

02

Hybrid Artist

A distinct AI character brought to life by real human performers. The performers interpret the character; their own identities remain separate from it.

03

100% AI

A fully generated character identity. Human creators shape its voice, stories and world. The term describes the character, not an absence of human authorship.
